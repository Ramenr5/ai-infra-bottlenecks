It’s the world’s worst kept secret that Nvidia is about to announce its own Arm-powered laptop chips at Computex this weekend, and now Microsoft, Nvidia, and Arm are all openly teasing the announcement. The Windows and Nvidia GeForce accounts on X both posted “A new era of PC” [earlier today](https://www.theverge.com/news/939960/microsoft-computex-teaser-surface-nvidia-windows-rumors), and now Arm has followed up with an [identical post](https://x.com/Arm/status/2060483398746214837).

All three posts include coordinates pointing to where Computex is hosted in Taipei. Nvidia is holding a [Computex keynote](https://www.nvidia.com/en-tw/gtc/taipei/keynote/?regcode=no-ncid&ncid=no-ncid) in Taipei at 8PM PT / 11PM ET on Sunday night, where it’s rumored to be announcing its new N1 and N1x laptop chips.

These Arm-powered Nvidia processors have been long-rumored, with reports [earlier this year](https://www.theverge.com/games/867056/leak-nvidia-n1-n1x-laptops-lenovo-dell) suggesting that both Lenovo and Dell have been preparing new laptops with the N1X chips. We first heard rumors about Nvidia’s laptop processors [in 2023](https://www.theverge.com/2023/10/23/23929240/nvidia-amd-cpu-arm-pc-chips-2025-release-rumors), and Dell CEO Michael Dell hinted at the possibility of an AI PC with Nvidia during an interview in 2024.

Nvidia’s entry into Windows on Arm will mean Qualcomm will no longer have an exclusive license for Microsoft’s Windows 11 Arm variant of its operating system. That’s good news for laptop competition, even if Qualcomm is trying to keep entry-level laptops affordable with its [new Snapdragon C platform](https://www.theverge.com/tech/938665/qualcomm-promises-300-windows-laptops-with-new-snapdragon-c).

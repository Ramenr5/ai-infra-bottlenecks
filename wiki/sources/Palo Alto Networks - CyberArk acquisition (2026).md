# Palo Alto Networks - CyberArk acquisition (2026)

**Type:** search-sourced M&A note. **Figures VERIFY.**

## What happened
- **Palo Alto Networks closed its ~$25B acquisition of CyberArk on 2026-02-11** (regulatory clearances US/EU/UK/Israel) — the **largest cybersecurity deal to date**, making **identity** a core PANW pillar ([FinancialContent](https://markets.financialcontent.com/stocks/article/marketminute-2026-2-12-palo-alto-networks-completes-historic-25-billion-cyberark-acquisition-to-solidify-identity-security-pillar), [GovConWire](https://www.govconwire.com/articles/palo-alto-networks-cyberark-25b-acquisition)).
- Explicit rationale: **"secure every identity — human, machine, and agentic."** CyberArk = the PAM / machine-identity leader.
- **2026-05-12: PANW launched Idira** — a next-gen identity platform unifying **human + machine + AI-agent identity** under a single privileged-access framework, extending CyberArk tech ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/12/idira-launches-palo-alto-networks-extends-cyberark-tech-machine-agentic-identities/)).
- Contribution: **Q3 FY26 NGS ARR +60% YoY to $8.1B**, incl. **~$1.6B from CyberArk + Chronosphere**.

## Why it matters (thesis)
- **The single biggest proof that identity is the new perimeter in the AI-agent era** — an incumbent paying $25B to own the identity chokepoint as machine identities head to 45-100:1 vs humans ([[Identity security in the AI era (2026)]]). Anchors L5 of the [AI cybersecurity narrative](../../narratives/2026-06-02 AI cybersecurity.md).
- Positions [[Palo Alto Networks]] as the **identity consolidator** vs [[CrowdStrike]] (Next-Gen Identity), [[Okta]] (Okta for AI Agents), and Microsoft Entra — the contested fight for the agent-identity standard.
- **Caveat:** the $25B price is rich → the theme is already partly priced; integration risk on the largest-ever cyber deal.

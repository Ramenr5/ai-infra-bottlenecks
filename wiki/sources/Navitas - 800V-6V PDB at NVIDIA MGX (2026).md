# Navitas - 800V-6V PDB at NVIDIA MGX (2026)

**Type:** search-sourced product/partnership note (Navitas press + coverage, 2026-06-03). **Figures VERIFY.**

## What happened
- **Navitas Semiconductor (Nasdaq: NVTS)** joined NVIDIA's **AI Factory MGX** partner ecosystem for **800 VDC** AI infrastructure (Partner Ceremony May 29 2026, Taipei); showcased its **800 V-to-6 V GaNFast Power Delivery Board (PDB)** at Computex 2026 (first debuted at GTC 2026) ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/03/3306044/0/en/navitas-collaborates-with-nvidia-mgx-ecosystem-to-accelerate-800-vdc-ai-infrastructure.html), [Navitas](https://navitassemi.com/navitas-debuts-revolutionary-800-v-6-v-power-delivery-board-at-nvidia-gtc-2026/)).
- The PDB **eliminates the traditional 48 V intermediate bus converter (IBC) stage** in the compute tray → single-stage 800V→point-of-load, freeing space + boosting efficiency.

## The board (specs — VERIFY)
- **97.5% peak efficiency**, **1 MHz** switching, **2100 W/in³** power density; ultra-thin (~20% thinner than a phone).
- **16 GaNFast FETs** (650 V, 11 mΩ, DFN8×8 dual-cooled packages).

## Financials (VERIFY)
- **Q1 FY26 revenue $8.6M** (beat ~$8.18M est); **loss/share −$0.15** (worse than −$0.05 est).
- Recently raised **$122M** via at-the-market equity — earmarked for high-power / AI-infrastructure push.
- Stock ~+24% pre-market on the NVIDIA 800V association ("NVIDIA lifts another stock").

## Why it matters (thesis)
- A new **GaN semiconductor enabler** of the [[800VDC transition]] — specifically the **on-blade / point-of-load DC-DC** that **Phase 2 (800VDC-native compute, Kyber)** requires; the 800V→6V single-stage is the density unlock.
- Slots Navitas into NVIDIA's MGX power ecosystem beside [[Power Integrations]] (GaN, NVIDIA-collaborating) and [[Infineon]] — but as a **sub-scale, loss-making pure-play** (high beta, dilution risk), not a derisked toll. **Showcase ≠ locked socket at volume.**

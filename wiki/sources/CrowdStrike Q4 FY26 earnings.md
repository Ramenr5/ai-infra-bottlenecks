# CrowdStrike Q4 FY26 earnings

**Publisher:** CrowdStrike IR + SEC 8-K; Motley Fool transcript
**Date:** reported 2026-03-03 (FY26 ended ~Jan 31 2026)
**URLs:** [CRWD IR](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/) · [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001535527/000153552726000007/crwd-20260303xex991.htm) · [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/03/03/crowdstrike-crwd-q4-2026-earnings-transcript/)

## Headline

First pure-play cyber software company past **$5B ending ARR** ($5.25B, +24%), with record Q4 net-new ARR ($331M, +47%) — and the growth story is increasingly **agentic security**: securing AI agents (non-human identities) and defending against AI-capable attackers.

## Agentic-AI / AI-security signals

- **Falcon Shield NAAR +300% YoY** — non-human / agentic identity protection
- **Charlotte AI** agent usage +6× YoY; its ARR tripling
- **AIDR** (AI Detection & Response) +5× sequential since launch
- Endpoint AI detection tracking **1,800+ AI apps / 160M unique instances** across the customer base
- Falcon Flex $1.69B ARR (+120%) — consumption model

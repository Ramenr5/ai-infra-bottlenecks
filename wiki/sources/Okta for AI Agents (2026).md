# Okta for AI Agents (2026)

**Type:** search-sourced product/earnings note. **Figures VERIFY.**

## What happened
- **Okta for AI Agents** reached **GA on 2026-04-30** — a platform to **discover sanctioned + unsanctioned AI agents, treat them as governed identities, and apply lifecycle controls** ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/03/04/okta-tops-earnings-revenue-expectations-identity-platform-targets-ai-agents/)). Since expanded to **Amazon Bedrock + non-Okta identity providers**.
- Okta **FY26 revenue $2.919B (+12% YoY)**; Q1 FY27 (reported 2026-05-28) beat on **agentic-AI demand**, stock +8%. Turnaround story (from ~$850M losses to ~$760M profit).

> [!warning] The honest caveat
> Okta management characterizes **AI-agent revenue as a FY2028-2029 story** — the agentic-identity TAM is real but **not yet a material revenue contributor**. The product validates the *theme*; monetization lags.

## Why it matters (thesis)
- A third independent incumbent (with [[Palo Alto Networks]]+CyberArk and [[CrowdStrike]] Next-Gen Identity) repositioning identity around the **agent-as-governed-identity** model → corroborates **L5 of the [AI cybersecurity narrative](../../narratives/2026-06-02 AI cybersecurity.md)** (identity = the new perimeter).
- Okta is the **pure-play identity** name — highest leverage to the agent-identity shift, but also most exposed if Microsoft Entra / PANW / CRWD capture the standard. Beaten-down stock = the theme is *less* priced here than in PANW.

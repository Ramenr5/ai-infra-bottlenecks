# Open-weight frontier parity (H1 2026)

**Type:** consolidated search-sourced note on open-weight model progress, H1 2026. **Tag figures VERIFY** — secondary coverage (leaderboards, recap blogs); confirm against primary benchmarks.

## The headline
- **Strongest open-weight model now within ~5% of the strongest closed-frontier** on code/reasoning (LiveCodeBench, Codeforces, Putnam-style formal reasoning). General-knowledge / hardest-retrieval gaps persist ([digitalapplied recap](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/open-weight-models-h1-2026-retrospective-deepseek-qwen-llama)).
- The buyer question shifted from *"is open-weight good enough?"* to **"which open-weight, which hosting stack, which workloads"** — a procurement-level commoditization signal, not a benchmark-table one.

## The spring-2026 release cluster (the inflection)
Within a ~30-day window: **OpenAI GPT-5.5**, **Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7**, **Google Gemini 3.5 Flash**, **DeepSeek V4 Pro** (MIT license + ~**75% price cut**), **Alibaba Qwen 3.7 Max** (surprise benchmark wins).
- **DeepSeek** — single architectural reset (V4) at frontier-adjacent quality, permissive license, aggressive price.
- **Qwen** — fastest cadence (3.5 Feb, 3.6 Apr, 3.7 Max), benchmark leadership among open weights.
- **Meta / Llama — ship-paused** open-weight, pivoting frontier effort to a **closed "Muse" line** — a notable defection from the open camp.
- Cost-per-token spread across open models **$0.04–$0.31** ([Spheron](https://www.spheron.network/blog/deepseek-vs-llama-4-vs-qwen3/)).

## Why it matters
- This is the **swing factor** named in both [[AI value capture across the stack]] and [[Future progression of AI (2026 view)]]: if open weights reach frontier parity, the **model layer commoditizes** → value migrates *down* to infra tolls and *out* to applications + cheap-compute operators.
- China-led (DeepSeek/Qwen) parity also feeds the [[DeepSeek]] / China-stack and [[Unitree Robotics]] geopolitical-divergence threads.

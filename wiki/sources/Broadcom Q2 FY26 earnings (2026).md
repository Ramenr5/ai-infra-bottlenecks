# Broadcom Q2 FY26 earnings (2026)

**Type:** search-sourced earnings note (reported 2026-06-03; Motley Fool transcript + 8-K + CNBC). **Figures VERIFY.**

## Headline numbers
- **AI semiconductor revenue $10.8B (+143% YoY)**; **Q3 guide ~$16.0B (+200% YoY)** — accelerating.
- **Networking ≈ 40% of AI semi revenue** — and the **rich-margin** part ("AI networking has very rich margins").
- **Record operating margin 67%**; adj EBITDA ~69%.
- **AI bookings >$30B** in the quarter; **order visibility now extends to 2028**, with **2028 a "substantial" step up from 2027**.
- **>$100B AI semi revenue FY2027 — reiterated, NOT raised** (the "market wanted a raise → +0% week" reaction).

## The quotes that matter
- **"Demand for XPUs and networking is simply insatiable."**
- **Networking = rich margins; custom XPU/TPU margins compress at scale** ("as TPUs accelerate, pressure overall on margins… the networking side has very rich margins"). → the value-capture tell.
- **Content-per-GW rising:** "embedding CPU cores into the same XPUs… multi-die with lots of HBM" → more $ content per gigawatt.

# AI eats SaaS — the seat-erosion debate (2026)

**Publishers:** IDC FutureScape, Bain & Co., CIO.com, Fortune, The Information; Nadella commentary
**URLs:** [Fortune "SaaSpocalypse"](https://fortune.com/2026/04/17/ai-saas-enterprise-software-moats-margins-saaspocalypse/) · [CIO "Is AI the end of SaaS?"](https://www.cio.com/article/4146669/is-ai-the-end-of-saas-as-we-know-it.html) · [Fortune — agents aren't killing SaaS but incumbents can't sleep](https://fortune.com/2026/02/10/ai-agents-anthropic-openai-arent-killing-saas-salesforce-servicenow-microsoft-workday-cant-sleep-easy/) · [Fortune — NOW earnings vs anti-SaaS vibes](https://fortune.com/2026/04/23/servicenow-earnings-forecast-blistering-growth-ai-product-sales/)

## Headline — the disintermediation risk is real, market-moving, and two-sided

The "AI eats SaaS" thesis is **sourced, not vibes**: agents threaten the **seat-based** model that made enterprise software profitable. But it's **not** a clean kill — incumbents like [[ServiceNow]] are adapting to consumption/outcome pricing and capturing agent usage. The overhang depresses multiples even on strong earnings.

## The bear case (sourced)

- **Nadella (late 2024): "SaaS is dead"** — "the way to think about the per-user business is not just per user, it's **per agent**"
- **IDC FutureScape Agentic AI 2026:** by **2028 pure seat-based pricing obsolete; 70% of vendors refactor** pricing to consumption/outcomes/capability
- **Bain:** "if an agent replaces a human task, customers will expect to pay based on **outcomes, not log-ons**"
- **"SaaSpocalypse"** rout wiped **>$2T** off tech (incl. a $357B one-day Microsoft drop) — the fear is priced
- **Fortune (Apr 2026):** ServiceNow's strong earnings *"fail to shift AI-driven anti-SaaS vibes"* — the narrative overhang is real even when fundamentals are good

## The bull / adaptation case (sourced)

- **[[ServiceNow]] hybrid pricing:** seat licenses + **metered "assists"** (consumption) on top — capturing agent value rather than losing it; moving newer AI offerings to consumption/value-based
- ServiceNow **Autonomous Workforce** (AI "specialists" running whole processes) + automating its own L1 service-desk = leaning into the shift
- **Fortune (Feb 2026):** "AI agents from Anthropic/OpenAI **aren't killing SaaS**" — but Salesforce/ServiceNow/Microsoft/Workday "can't sleep easy"
- Salesforce parallel: Agentforce usage-based + "all-you-can-eat" AELA, $2/conversation

## Why it matters for the thesis (narrative gap filled)

This is the **disintermediation risk** flagged in the [CRWD+NOW narrative](../../narratives/2026-06-01 CRWD %2B NOW.md) — previously "under-sourced," now sourced. Net read: **the seat-based model erodes, but the agent/consumption layer is up for grabs** — and NOW is positioning to capture it. The bear isn't "NOW dies," it's "NOW's revenue *mix* and *multiple* reset as seats convert to metered agent usage, with execution + pricing risk in the transition." Directly pressures the narrative's **tailwind > threat** directional bet.

# Goldman - Humanoid robot ecosystem map (2026)

**Type:** research exhibit (Goldman-style "Exhibit 2: Mapping of humanoid robot technology enablers"), shared as an image. **Names verbatim from the chart; financials not given — VERIFY.**

## What it maps
**~40 robot makers** vs **~80 technology enablers**, color-coded by country; **grey = privately owned**.

- **Makers (mostly private + Chinese):** 1X, Agility, Apptronik, Figure AI, Sanctuary, Boston Dynamics (Hyundai), NEURA, Fourier, + China bloc (Unitree, UBTECH, AGIBOT, Robot Era, Galbot, LimX, Leju, Kepler…). **Listed:** Tesla (TSLA), BYD (002594.SZ), Xiaomi (1810.HK), XPENG (XPEV), GAC (601238.SS), CATL (300750.SZ), Estun/Codroid (002747.SZ).
- **Enablers by sub-system:** foundation models (NVIDIA/Tesla/Alphabet/Meta/OpenAI/iFlytek/Huawei); GPU/CPU (NVIDIA/Intel); **rotary actuators** (Harmonic Drive, LeaderDrive, Shuanghuan, Reach Machinery, Zhongda Leader, FORE); **linear actuators** (Schaeffler, Sanhua, Hengli, Beite, XCC, LeaderDrive, Zhenyu, Shuanglin); **dexterous hands** (Maxon, LeaderDrive, Inovance, Leadshine, RoboSense, Fortior, Paxini); actuator assembly (Sanhua, Tuopu); sensors (Novanta, Vishay Precision, Murata, TDK, Kunwei); **vision/LiDAR** (Hesai, RoboSense, Sunny Optical, Luminar, Innoviz, DJI/Livox, Velodyne, Intel, Orbbec); batteries (CATL, Samsung SDI, Panasonic).

## Why it matters (thesis)
- **Confirms "own the enablers, not the makers":** the makers are mostly private/Chinese; the *enablers* are where the listed, investable picks-and-shovels exposure sits ([[Physical AI & robotics]]).
- **LeaderDrive appears in ALL THREE actuator categories** (rotary + linear + dexterous) — the Chinese multi-actuator champion vs Japan's rotary-only [[Harmonic Drive Systems]].
- **China dominates the humanoid supply chain even more than AI-compute** (makers + components) — supply/geopolitical read.
- **Cross-overs:** vision = the **AV-LiDAR** names; batteries = the **EV** names — humanoid rides existing AV/EV component tolls.

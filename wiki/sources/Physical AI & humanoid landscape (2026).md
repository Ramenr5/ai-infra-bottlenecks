## The read (for the layer page)
- **The toll-keeper is still NVIDIA** — full-stack physical-AI platform (Jetson Thor compute, Isaac GROOT, Cosmos world model, Drive/Alpamayo AV) captures value *across* every robot maker, the same picks-and-shovels logic as [[AI value capture across the stack]].
- **Pure-play humanoid equities are scarce** — most leaders (Figure, 1X, Unitree, Apptronik, AgiBot) are **private**; the main public direct-exposure name is **[[Tesla]]**. Public investors mostly get physical AI through the *enablers* (compute, actuators, sensors, motion control).
- **China is scaling on units, the West on capability/valuation** — Unitree/AgiBot ship volume cheaply; Figure/1X raise on capability. A supply-vs-IP split worth watching.

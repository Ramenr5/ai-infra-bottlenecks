# Menlo Ventures — 2025 State of Generative AI in the Enterprise

**Publisher:** Menlo Ventures (VC; annual enterprise-AI spend survey)
**Published:** Dec 2025
**URL:** [menlovc.com/.../2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/) · [PDF](https://menlovc.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/menlo_ventures_enterprise_ai_report-2025-123125.pdf)

## Headline — where enterprise AI dollars actually go

**$37B enterprise GenAI spend in 2025 (3.2× YoY), split roughly 50/50 between the application layer (~$19B) and the infrastructure layer ($18B).** This is the cleanest counter-data to the "AI labs capture *all* the value" framing — the application layer (where [[CrowdStrike]] / [[ServiceNow]] live) captured about half.

## Spend split (2025)

| Layer | Spend | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Application layer** | **~$19B** | the apps enterprises actually buy |
| **Infrastructure layer** | **$18B** | Foundation-model APIs **$12.5B** + model-training infra $4.0B + AI infra (orchestration/data) $1.5B |

## Model-lab share (the concentration within infra)

- **[[Anthropic]] = 40% of enterprise LLM spend** (up from 24% in 2024, 12% in 2023) — unseated OpenAI as enterprise leader
- **OpenAI = 27%** (down from 50% in 2023)

## Why it matters for the thesis

- **Resolves the value-capture contradiction (partly):** [[SemiAnalysis - AI Value Capture (2026)]] said "labs capture all the value." Menlo says **the application layer captured ~half of enterprise AI spend** — so the read-through that app-layer incumbents (CRWD/NOW) capture real value is **sourced, not just inferred** (directly strengthens the [narrative](../../narratives/2026-06-01 CRWD %2B NOW.md) L6).
- **But the nuance cuts both ways:** the app half is **fragmented** across many vendors, while the infra half concentrates — Anthropic alone takes 40% of the foundation-model-API bucket. So *labs capture a concentrated share*; *apps capture a larger-but-fragmented share*. CRWD/NOW are in the value-capturing half but compete in a crowded field.
- **Anthropic 40% enterprise LLM share** corroborates [[SemiAnalysis - Anthropic Bedrock AWS Margins (2026)]] / [[Anthropic]] (Anthropic > OpenAI in enterprise).

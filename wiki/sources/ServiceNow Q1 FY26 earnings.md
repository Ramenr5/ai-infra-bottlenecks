# ServiceNow Q1 FY26 earnings

**Publisher:** ServiceNow IR + SEC 8-K; Motley Fool transcript
**Date:** reported 2026-04-22 (Q1 ended ~Mar 31 2026)
**URLs:** [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001373715/000137371526000054/erq1fy26.htm) · [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/22/servicenow-now-q1-2026-earnings-transcript/)

## Headline

Subscription revenue $3.671B (+19% cc), beat the high end, with **AI-specific 2026 commitments revised UP 50% to $1.5B** — and CEO Bill McDermott explicitly framing ServiceNow as the **"quarterback"** orchestrating which agent/LLM runs each enterprise workflow.

## Agentic-AI signals

- **AI commitments → $1.5B for 2026** (raised from $1B, +50%)
- **Now Assist** >$600M ACV → $1B target; deals with 3+ Now Assist products +70% YoY; 36 deals with 5+ products
- **Agent control-plane positioning** — McDermott: ServiceNow *"figures out which agent or LLM to use,"* governance end-to-end ("the quarterback")
- **Armis acquisition** — moves NOW into securing the agentic enterprise (overlaps [[CrowdStrike]])

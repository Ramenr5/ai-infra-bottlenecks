# AI factory tokenomics (2026)

**Type:** consolidated search-sourced note on the AI-factory / token-economics framing (NVIDIA GTC 2026 + Morgan Stanley + SemiAnalysis modeling). **Tag figures VERIFY** — mix of NVIDIA marketing, analyst models, and secondary coverage; confirm before acting.

## Jensen's formula + the unit economics
- **Revenue = (Tokens per Watt) × (Available Gigawatts)** — the AI-factory operating equation. Because a factory is **GW-capped**, **tokens-per-watt becomes the single most important metric for the top line** ([Morgan Stanley](https://www.morganstanley.com/insights/articles/nvidia-jensen-huang-compute-new-economic-engine-tmt-2026), [NVIDIA dev blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/scaling-token-factory-revenue-and-ai-efficiency-by-maximizing-performance-per-watt/)).
- **$100B in a 1-GW AI factory → up to $150B/yr token revenue** (NVIDIA framing).
- **Token price tiers (NVIDIA, on stage):** low ~**$1/M tokens**, mid **$3–6**, high/engineering **~$45**, premium real-time higher ([FinancialContent](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/marketminute-2026-3-20-the-token-factory-jensen-huang-reimagines-the-ai-economy-with-nvidias-newest-category)).
- Hardware leverage: **Blackwell ~35× throughput** vs prior; **Vera Rubin moves premium tiers another order of magnitude** (~10× lower cost/token).

## Morgan Stanley 100-MW "AI inference factory" model (VERIFY)
- **Capex ~$660M per 100 MW** (datacenter + power), depreciated **~10 yr**.
- **Annual TCO $330M–$807M** per 100 MW.
- Reference token price **$0.20/M tokens** (vs OpenAI/Gemini APIs), **~70% utilization**.
- **Profit margin >50%** achievable — and *"whether using NVIDIA or Huawei chips"* (the economics work across silicon) ([Futu/MS](https://news.futunn.com/en/post/60704991/morgan-stanley-s-modeling-of-the-ai-inference-factory-whether)).
- NVIDIA's own example: **$5M GB200 NVL72 → ~$75M DeepSeek-R1 token revenue = ~15× return** on hardware at current token prices.

## Corroborating margin / demand signals
- **Anthropic inference gross margin 38% → >70%** ([[SemiAnalysis - Anthropic Bedrock AWS Margins (2026)]]).
- **1-yr H100 rental +40% off the Oct-2025 bottom** — rental prices *rising*, which rebuts the depreciation-bear ([[Thesis stress-tests]], Burry row).

## The skeptic side (keep both)
- **"Jensen's not telling the whole story about AI tokenomics"** (Rob Manson, Medium) — the formula ignores demand elasticity, competition driving token prices down, and that *someone* must buy $150B of tokens per GW.
- **SemiAnalysis "AI Dark Output"** — reasoning models burn (and bill) large hidden "thinking" token volumes; tokens-per-watt flatters revenue if much output is invisible/low-value.
- The deeper point: tokens-per-watt is a **supply-side** metric. It says nothing about whether **demand** clears at those prices — the core [[Capex bust scenario]] question.

- This is the **organizing economic frame** of the 2026 buildout — converts the [[Power & energy]] constraint directly into a revenue function, and reframes capex as factory capex (output-measured), not IT spend.

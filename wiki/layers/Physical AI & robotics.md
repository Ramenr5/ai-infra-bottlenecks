# Physical AI & robotics

**The layer where AI leaves the data center and enters machines** — humanoids, autonomous vehicles, industrial robots. Positioned at the NVIDIA Computex 2026 keynote as **the next demand driver** after agentic AI (Phase 4 of [[Future progression of AI (2026 view)]]). The investment relevance: it extends the AI compute curve into a **$40T labor-automation TAM** (Jensen's framing) and opens a second demand front *beyond* hyperscaler data centers.

## Why it matters to the thesis
- **Second demand front for compute.** Robots need training (world models, simulation, synthetic data) AND on-board inference (edge accelerators) — both feed the [[Compute & silicon]] demand curve. NVIDIA already books **>$9B physical-AI revenue TTM** ([[Physical AI & humanoid landscape (2026)]]).
- **Same value-capture logic, new domain.** Per [[AI value capture across the stack]]: the durable rent goes to the **full-stack toll-keeper**, not the robot assembler. NVIDIA's Jetson Thor + Isaac GROOT + Cosmos + Drive/Alpamayo stack is to robots what its GPU stack is to data centers — captures value across every maker.

## The NVIDIA physical-AI stack (the toll layer)
From [[NVIDIA Computex 2026 Keynote]]:
- **Jetson Thor** — on-board robotics compute (the "brain" inside the robot).
- **Isaac GROOT** — reference humanoid platform (NVIDIA + Unitree body) + the GROOT foundation models.
- **Cosmos 3** — open robotics **world model** for simulation / synthetic-data generation (training without real-world data collection).
- **Alpamayo** — "world's first reasoning autonomous vehicle" on **Drive Hyperion**.
- Read-through: NVIDIA sells the **training cloud + the simulation + the on-board chip** — three tolls per robot. → [[NVIDIA]]

## The robot makers (demand for the stack)
- **Public, direct:** [[Tesla]] (Optimus — Gen 3 scaling at Fremont; the main listed pure-play).
- **Private leaders:** [[Figure AI]] ($39B val; 40-unit fleet at BMW), [[Unitree Robotics]] (China volume leader, 5,500+ units '25), Apptronik ($5.5B val), 1X (NEO home robot), AgiBot (China).
- **Split to watch:** China ships *units* cheaply (Unitree/AgiBot); the West raises on *capability/valuation* (Figure/1X). Supply-scale vs IP. ([[Physical AI & humanoid landscape (2026)]])

## Where the rent lands (tradable read)
- **Highest conviction — the enabling tolls** (capture value across all robot makers, like owning the picks): [[NVIDIA]] (compute + sim + edge), plus the analog/sensor/actuator/motion-control suppliers (candidate pages: power semis [[Infineon]], [[Power Integrations]]; motion/sensing — to build).
- **Direct but concentrated:** [[Tesla]] — the only large-cap pure-play; binary on Optimus execution.
- **Mostly private:** the humanoid pure-plays (Figure, 1X, Unitree, Apptronik) — venture/pre-IPO exposure only; watch for the first humanoid IPO as a regime marker.
- **Bear / what would break it:** physical AI stays demo-ware (pilots don't scale to fleets); the $40T TAM is a *decade+* story, not a 2026-2028 one — so near-term it's **option value, not order book** (contrast the data-center side, which *is* an order book — [[Capex trajectory thesis]]).

## Leading indicators
- Humanoid **fleet deployments** scaling (Figure@BMW 40 → hundreds → thousands); first humanoid **IPO**.
- NVIDIA **physical-AI revenue** run-rate (>$9B TTM and climbing = confirm).
- Unit **cost-down** curve (sub-$20k humanoids) — the Jevons trigger for robots.
- AV **reasoning** deployments (Alpamayo/Drive) moving from pilot to commercial.

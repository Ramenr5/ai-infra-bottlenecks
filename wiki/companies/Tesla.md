# Tesla

**The main listed pure-play on the [[Physical AI & robotics]] thesis** — via the **Optimus** humanoid and its FSD/Dojo autonomy stack. For this wiki Tesla matters less as an automaker and more as the one large-cap public name with **direct humanoid exposure**.

## Physical-AI relevance
- **Optimus** humanoid — Gen 3 production converting at the Fremont facility; target unit cost ~**$20-30k** at scale (as of 2026, VERIFY — [[Physical AI & humanoid landscape (2026)]]).
- Vertically integrated: own actuators, own autonomy silicon (Dojo / FSD inference), own manufacturing — a different model from the [[NVIDIA]]-stack robot makers ([[Figure AI]], [[Unitree Robotics]]) who buy the brain.
- The bet: Tesla can apply auto-scale manufacturing to humanoids. The risk: **binary on Optimus execution + timeline** — repeatedly guided, not yet shipped at volume.

## Investment read
- **Direct but concentrated** physical-AI exposure (per [[AI value capture across the stack]], the robot *assembler* is a thinner-rent position than the full-stack toll-keeper [[NVIDIA]]).
- Contrast: NVIDIA captures rent across *every* robot maker; Tesla captures only its own — but owns the whole stack if Optimus works.
- Watch: Optimus fleet deployments (internal Tesla factories first), unit-cost curve, any external Optimus sales.

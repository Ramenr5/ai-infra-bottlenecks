# MediaTek

Taiwanese fabless SoC designer (TWSE: 2454). NVIDIA's **co-development partner for the N1/N1X Arm client SoCs** (the "new era of PC" — per [[NVIDIA Computex 2026 Keynote]] + The Verge), and prior collaborator on NVIDIA's Grace-class CPU/automotive efforts.

## Key facts (verify before acting)
- N1/N1X Arm laptop chips: NVIDIA GPU IP + MediaTek SoC integration; OEMs **Lenovo + Dell** prepping laptops; breaks **Qualcomm's** Windows-on-Arm exclusivity
- CEO Rick Tsai featured in NVIDIA's GTC Taipei "PCs to Embedded AI" pregame

## Why it matters
- Picks-and-shovels exposure to NVIDIA's **client/PC** expansion (distinct from datacenter); Windows-on-Arm competition opens

## Risks
- N1X execution/timing; competition (Qualcomm Snapdragon, AMD, Intel); rumor-stage details

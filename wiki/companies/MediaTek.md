# MediaTek

Taiwanese fabless SoC designer (TWSE: 2454) — exposure to AI on **two** fronts: NVIDIA's **client/PC** SoC partner *and* Google's **datacenter custom-ASIC (TPU)** partner.

**Front 1 — client PC:** NVIDIA's **co-development partner for the N1/N1X Arm client SoCs** (the "new era of PC" — [[NVIDIA Computex 2026 Keynote]]); prior Grace-class CPU/automotive collaborator.

## Front 2 — Google TPU (the datacenter beneficiary thread)
- **MediaTek co-develops Google's Ironwood (TPU v7) alongside [[Broadcom]]** (on [[TSMC]] N3P). Google's multi-partner split (per coverage, VERIFY): **Broadcom = training, MediaTek = inference, [[Marvell Technology]] = edge/memory** — i.e. Google's **"diversity of sources"** to create pricing competition and avoid single-sourcing Broadcom ([[Broadcom Q2 FY26 earnings (2026)]]).
- This makes MediaTek a **primary beneficiary of Google's ASIC diversification** — a datacenter custom-silicon revenue stream distinct from (and larger-TAM than) the N1X client angle.
- MediaTek CEO flagged **memory ≈ 50% of XPU cost** (Feb 2026) → ties XPU economics to [[HBM allocation]] / content-per-GW.

## Key facts (verify before acting)
- N1/N1X Arm laptop chips: NVIDIA GPU IP + MediaTek SoC integration; OEMs **Lenovo + Dell** prepping laptops; breaks **Qualcomm's** Windows-on-Arm exclusivity
- CEO Rick Tsai featured in NVIDIA's GTC Taipei "PCs to Embedded AI" pregame

## Why it matters
- **Two AI revenue fronts:** NVIDIA client/PC (N1X) *and* Google datacenter TPU (inference). The TPU thread is the bigger-TAM, more thesis-relevant one — direct exposure to the [[Networking & interconnect|custom-ASIC]] ramp and Google's anti-Broadcom-concentration push.
- Picks-and-shovels on *both* NVIDIA's client expansion and the hyperscaler-custom-silicon wave.

## Risks
- N1X execution/timing; competition (Qualcomm Snapdragon, AMD, Intel)
- On TPU: a *second-source* role (Broadcom remains Google's lead) — share/volume uncertain; Google could re-consolidate

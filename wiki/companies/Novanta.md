# Novanta

**The force/torque-sensing toll** (Nasdaq: NOVT) — owns **ATI Industrial Automation**, the leader in **6-axis force/torque sensors** that give robots a sense of touch. A Tier-1 *sensing* chokepoint in [[Physical AI & robotics]].

## The thesis
- **6-axis force/torque feedback is dexterity-critical** — humanoids/mobile-manipulators need it for fine manipulation, assembly, safe interaction. ATI's Varo + Nano/Mini sensors are purpose-built for it.
- Goldman lists **ATI (Novanta)** under sensors (high tech-barrier) — a concentrated, high-barrier sensing toll robust to which-maker-wins.
- Novanta is a diversified photonics/precision-motion/medical company; robotics-sensing is the AI growth vector.

## Investment read — Tier-1 sensing toll (Western, US-listed)
- High-barrier, concentrated force/torque sensing → cleaner toll than the commoditizing motor/LiDAR layers. Bull: dexterity demand scales sensor count per robot. Bear: humanoid volumes still tiny (option value); sensing is a small slice of a diversified company.

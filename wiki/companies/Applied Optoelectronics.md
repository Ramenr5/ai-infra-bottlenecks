# Applied Optoelectronics

**The vertically-integrated US optical-transceiver independent** (Nasdaq: AAOI) — makes its own lasers + 800G/1.6T datacenter transceivers. A high-beta way to play [[Networking & interconnect]] optics, differentiated by **US capacity + vertical integration** (vs the NVIDIA-anointed [[Lumentum]]/[[Coherent]]).

## Key figures (2026, VERIFY)
- **~100k 800G units/mo (Q1'26) → targeting >500k/mo (800G+1.6T) by end-2026**; Houston footprint → ~900k sq ft; **laser capacity +350% by 2027**.
- **<10% of 800G/1.6T component value from China** (Q4'25) — vertical integration + US manufacturing de-risks supply/policy.
- **$324M+ backlog**; first **volume 1.6T order** from a major hyperscaler.

## The thesis
- **Demand:** 800G units 24M (2025) → ~63M (2026); 1.6T ramping (GB300 = 162 modules/cabinet). 800G *persists* for Tier-2/sovereign builds even as hyperscalers move to 1.6T.
- **The differentiator + hedge:** AAOI **makes its own lasers**. With NVIDIA having **locked up merchant EML supply** ($4B to Lumentum+Coherent, lead times past 2027 — see [[Networking & interconnect]]), AAOI's vertical integration is a *structural hedge* vs the EML bottleneck that constrains non-integrated assemblers.
- **The variant perception (under-modeled):** consensus extrapolates **ASP down** (~$1,200 '23 → ~$400 '26E, GM <25%). If the EML/component shortage bites H1, ASP could *firm or rise* → margin upside across AAOI/[[Lumentum]]/[[Coherent]] not in models.

## Risks
- **Execution is the swing factor** — AAOI's history of losses, dilution, lumpy hyperscaler orders, and missed ramps. "Double/triple *if they execute*."
- **Not the NVIDIA-anointed name** ([[Lumentum]]/[[Coherent]] got the capital/partnerships); AMD/NVDA talks are **rumor, not confirmed**.
- ASP decline resumes if components loosen; full valuation (~6.7× P/S); China module competition.

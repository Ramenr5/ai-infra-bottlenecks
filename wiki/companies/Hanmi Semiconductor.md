# Hanmi Semiconductor

**The incumbent HBM TC-bonder leader — facing diversification.** Korean maker of thermo-compression (TC) bonders that stack HBM die; the bonding-tool slice of [[Advanced packaging & OSAT (the AI packaging toll)]].

## Key figures (2026, VERIFY)
- **>90% share of HBM3E 12-layer production TC bonders** (~71% overall TC-bonder share); historically near-sole supplier to [[SK Hynix]].
- **BUT diversification risk is the story:** SK Hynix onboarding [[ASMPT]] for HBM3E/HBM4 TCB; Micron picking [[BESI]] as sole TCB for HBM4 → **Hanmi's SK Hynix share may fall to 20-30% by 2026** (the largest displacement). Patent clash with Hanwha.

## Investment read
- A live case study that **even a >90% incumbent toll is contestable** — the HBM makers *want* multi-sourcing to avoid single-supplier risk. Bull: HBM4 volume + Micron/China demand offsets. Bear: SK Hynix share erosion to ASMPT/BESI.

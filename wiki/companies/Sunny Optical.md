# Sunny Optical

**Optics/vision modules — the diversified vision toll** (HKEX: 2382). Lenses + camera modules across smartphone/auto/robotics; a robotic-vision solution provider (2D RGB, stereo, structured light, ToF) for [[Physical AI & robotics]].

## The thesis
- **Robotic vision** (3D localization, mapping, navigation, obstacle avoidance) for humanoids + AMRs; Goldman lists it under camera/LiDAR.
- **Diversified** optics franchise (smartphone + auto ADAS + robotics) → humanoid vision is a growth option on a real, cash-generative optics business — *less* binary than the pure LiDAR names.

## Investment read — Tier-2 (optics toll, HK-accessible)
- A **lower-beta, accessible (HK-listed)** way to play robot vision vs the competitive pure-LiDAR names ([[Hesai]]/RoboSense). Bull: optics content scales with robot cameras; diversified base. Bear: vision/optics is competitive + commoditizing at the low end; humanoid a small slice today.

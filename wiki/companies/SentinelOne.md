# SentinelOne

**Endpoint/XDR security with an autonomous-SecOps bet** (NYSE: S) — the AI-native challenger to [[CrowdStrike]], and the clearest embodiment of the narrative's **double-edged** AI dynamic.

## Key figures (FY2026, VERIFY)
- **FY26 revenue $1,001.3M (+22%)** — crossed $1B; **ARR +22% to $1,119.1M** (Jan 31 2026); non-GAAP profitable; margins +600bps.

## The double edge — Purple AI
- **Purple AI** = an **AI security analyst** that automates investigation/response/SecOps inside the Singularity platform → the **autonomous SOC**.
- This is exactly the narrative's **"security eats itself"** tension ([AI cybersecurity narrative](../../narratives/2026-06-02 AI cybersecurity.md), L8): AI *automating the defenders*. Bull = does more security work per dollar (margin + displacement of rivals' analyst-heavy models); bear = if security value migrates from headcount/seats to automated agents, it pressures the **seat-linked revenue model** of the whole sector ([[AI eats SaaS - the seat-erosion debate (2026)]]).

## Why it matters for the thesis
- The **AI-native disruptor** angle: SentinelOne argues AI-first architecture out-automates incumbents — a *threat to* [[CrowdStrike]]/[[Palo Alto Networks]] as much as a beneficiary of the tailwind.
- Sub-scale vs CRWD ($1.1B vs $5.25B ARR) — execution + competitive risk, but the autonomous-SOC thesis is its sharpest differentiator.

## Risks
- Intense competition (CRWD, Microsoft Defender, PANW Cortex); the autonomous-SOC bet cuts both ways for the sector's pricing model.

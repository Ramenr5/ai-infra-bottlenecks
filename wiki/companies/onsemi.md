# onsemi

**SiC/GaN power-semiconductor maker** (Nasdaq: ON; fka ON Semiconductor) — a [[800VDC transition]] enabler with a large auto/industrial core.

## The AI-power hook
- **NVIDIA 800VDC collaboration** (Jul 2025) + **expanded MGX-ecosystem role** (May 29 2026) for next-gen AI-datacenter power.
- **Power-semi content per 1 MW AI rack doubled $50k → $100k** (broad SiC + GaN portfolio) — the content-growth stat that matters.
- **Q1 2026 AI-datacenter business +30% sequential** — small base, fast growth.
- Slots into the [[800VDC transition]] **semiconductor-enablers** list (with [[Wolfspeed]], [[Infineon]], [[Power Integrations]], [[Navitas Semiconductor]]).

## Investment read — cyclical recovery + AI optionality
- The AI-datacenter piece is **real but small** relative to onsemi's **auto/industrial core**, which has been in an **EV-SiC downturn**. So this is a **cyclical recovery story with AI-power optionality**, not a pure AI play.
- Bull: $100k/MW content + MGX validation + cyclical trough recovery. Bear: auto/industrial drag dominates the P&L; SiC competition (Wolfspeed, Infineon, ST) + China SiC.

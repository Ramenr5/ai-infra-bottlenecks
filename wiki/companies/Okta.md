# Okta

**The pure-play identity name** (NASDAQ: OKTA) — IAM/SSO for workforce + customer identity, repositioning around the AI-agent era. Highest leverage to the agent-identity shift; also the most exposed if a platform giant captures the standard.

## Key figures (FY2026, VERIFY)
- **FY26 revenue $2.919B (+12% YoY)**; turnaround to ~$760M profit (from ~$850M losses).
- **Okta for AI Agents — GA 2026-04-30**: discovers sanctioned/unsanctioned AI agents, governs them as identities with lifecycle controls; expanded to Amazon Bedrock + non-Okta IdPs ([[Okta for AI Agents (2026)]]).
- Q1 FY27 (2026-05-28) beat on **agentic-AI demand**, stock +8%.

> [!warning] Monetization lags the theme
> Management frames **AI-agent revenue as a FY2028-2029 story** — the agentic-identity TAM is real but not yet a material contributor. The product validates the thesis; the revenue is forward.

## Why it matters for the thesis
- The **purest expression of "identity = the new perimeter"** ([AI cybersecurity narrative](../../narratives/2026-06-02 AI cybersecurity.md)) — if agent identity is the durable chokepoint, Okta has the most upside leverage.
- **Beaten-down stock** (was −23% on the year) → the agentic theme is *less* priced in here than in [[Palo Alto Networks]] — higher risk/reward, contingent on monetization arriving.

## Risks
- **Platform capture:** [[Palo Alto Networks]] (+CyberArk/Idira), [[CrowdStrike]] (Next-Gen Identity), and **Microsoft Entra** all chasing the same agent-identity layer — Okta lacks a broader platform to cross-sell/defend.
- Monetization timing (FY28-29) — a long wait for the thesis to pay.
- 2023 breach overhang on trust; Microsoft Entra bundling.

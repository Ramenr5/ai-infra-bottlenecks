# Schaeffler

**The Western integrated-actuator + roller-screw toll for humanoids** (Xetra: SHA). German precision-mechanical incumbent (bearings, ball/planetary roller screws) — the cleanest *Western* name in [[Physical AI & robotics]]'s Tier-1 mechanical chokepoints.

## The thesis hook
- **CES 2026 "all-in-one" humanoid actuator** — integrates **planetary roller screw + bearings + encoders + force sensors + frameless torque motor + servo drive + thermal management** in one unit (60-250 Nm). Targets the actuator *supply-chain bottleneck* directly.
- Goldman lists Schaeffler under **planetary roller screw** (a Tier-1 chokepoint — high qualification barrier, hip/knee high-torque).
- **Hexagon Robotics** partnership; deploying **hundreds of humanoids in its own factories** (demand + proof-point).

## Investment read — Tier-1 toll (Western)
- **High-barrier, multi-component, robust to which-maker-wins** — precision roller screws + bearings are hard to qualify and concentrated; the integrated actuator captures more BOM. The investable *Western* counter to China's [[LeaderDrive]] cost edge.
- Caveat: Schaeffler is a **large diversified auto/industrial** company — humanoids are a growth option on a cyclical core (like [[onsemi]] in power); China cost competition on the components.

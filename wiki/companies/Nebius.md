# Nebius

**AI-native neocloud** (NASDAQ: NBIS) — GPU/CPU capacity-on-demand competing with hyperscalers for AI workloads. A named **NVIDIA Vera CPU cloud** and DSX AI-factory partner ([[NVIDIA Computex 2026 Keynote - ServeTheHome Live (2026)]]).

## Why it matters (to the thesis)
- A **deployment vehicle** for the AI buildout — one of the neoclouds that physically racks NVIDIA GPUs/CPUs and rents them (alongside [[CoreWeave]], [[Lambda]], [[Nscale]], [[Crusoe Energy|Crusoe]]).
- Part of **Microsoft's $60B+ neocloud commitments** (with Nscale, CoreWeave, Iren, Lambda) — i.e. hyperscalers renting neocloud capacity to plug their own shortfall (VERIFY).
- **Vera demand channel:** named among the clouds adopting the [[CPU scaling (host-CPU ratio)|Vera CPU]] — neoclouds are how standalone Vera racks reach the market.

## Investment read
- Per [[AI value capture across the stack]], neoclouds are the **low-rent, capacity-constrained, debt-financed** tier — they carry the cyclical risk; the durable rent sits with the tolls they buy ([[NVIDIA]], HBM, [[TSMC]]). Trade the cycle, don't mistake backlog for margin.

# Teradyne

**Half of the semiconductor-test duopoly (with Advantest) — plus a robotics arm.** The cleanest "AI test toll" name ([[Semiconductor test (the AI test toll)]]): AI-chip complexity raises test intensity, and Teradyne sits on that toll.

## Key figures (Q1 2026, VERIFY)
- Revenue **$1.282B** — Semi Test **$1.111B** (crossed $1B for the first time), Robotics $91M, Product Test $80M.
- **~70% of revenue AI-related** (management).
- **>50% share of testing HBM wafer stacks** → direct [[HBM allocation]] read-through (HBM4 → taller stacks → more test).

## The thesis
- **Test toll:** ATE is a Teradyne/Advantest duopoly; AI raises test content per chip (HBM KGD, multi-die SLT, longer test times). Robust to the silicon war — every AI chip gets tested ([[Semiconductor test (the AI test toll)]]).
- **Free robotics option:** Universal Robots (cobots) + MiR (AMRs); rolling out **"Cognitive Cobots" with NVIDIA AI toolkits** → [[Physical AI & robotics]] exposure at no extra entry cost.
- **Optical-test optionality:** Quantifi Photonics deal → AI optical-I/O test as [[Networking & interconnect]] goes optical/CPO.

## Investment read
- **Quality AI-infra toll**, lower-consensus than NVIDIA/HBM. Conviction: high on the test thesis.
- **Caveats:** Advantest is the bigger *pure* HBM/SoC-test winner; test is **cyclical to chip volume** (a capex pause hits it); robotics has been a slow grower.

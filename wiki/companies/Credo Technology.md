# Credo Technology

**The active-electrical-cable (AEC) pioneer** (Nasdaq: CRDO) — the "$500 purple cables" connecting AI racks. The scale-up-copper sweet spot of [[Networking & interconnect]] (copper-with-DSP, between passive copper and optics).

## Key figures (2026, VERIFY)
- Q2 FY26 revenue **+272% YoY** — driven by **AWS + Microsoft** AI build-outs; returned to profit (~$268M quarter).
- **AEC market → ~$4B by 2028** (a category Credo created); fastest-growing line, diversifying customer base.

## Investment read
- A **high-growth, AI-pure** way to play scale-up interconnect — AECs win where passive copper can't reach and optics is overkill/too power-hungry. Bull: AEC adoption curve + hyperscaler design wins. Bear: **customer concentration** (AWS/MSFT), high-beta valuation, and architecture risk (optics/CPO could encroach on longer reaches).

# FormFactor

**The probe-card leader — the test *interface* toll.** Probe cards make the electrical contact for wafer test; FormFactor supplies the **top-3 HBM makers**. Part of [[Semiconductor test (the AI test toll)]].

## Key figures (2026, VERIFY)
- Q1 FY26 revenue **$226.1M** (from $171.4M) — driven by probe-card demand for **Foundry/Logic + HBM DRAM**.
- **HBM4 inflection:** higher layer count → higher test intensity → **probe cards wear out faster → faster replacement cycles** (a recurring-consumable tailwind). Reframed as a leveraged HBM + advanced-logic + custom-accelerator beneficiary.

## Investment read
- A **consumable-like test toll** (probe cards are replaced, not one-time) levered directly to HBM4 + AI-logic test intensity — robust to the silicon war. Bull: HBM4 wear cycle + custom-ASIC test. Bear: rich valuation; cyclical to wafer-test volume.

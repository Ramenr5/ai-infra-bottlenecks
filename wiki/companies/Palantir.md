# Palantir

**The ontology/operational-AI play in the [[Application & software]] layer** — AIP (Artificial Intelligence Platform) wraps LLMs around Palantir's data ontology so enterprises/governments deploy agents against *their own operational data*. The thesis fit: a **proprietary-data moat that agents can't route around** — exactly the [[Application & software]] selection rule.

## Why it matters
- **Data/ontology as the moat:** AIP's value is the structured operational context (the ontology), not the model — so it's relatively *insulated* from model-layer commoditization ([[Open-weight commoditization (the swing factor)]]). Cheaper/open models are an *input cost-down* for Palantir, not a competitive threat. A structurally favorable position in [[AI value capture across the stack]].
- **Government + commercial:** dual engine; US commercial AIP adoption is the growth narrative.

## Key figures (Q1 2026, reported 2026-05-04 — **primary**, per release coverage)
- **Revenue $1.6B, +85% YoY** — fastest growth since at least 2020.
- **US revenue +104% YoY** (crossed 100% for the first time); **US-commercial TTM TCV bookings $4.7B (+115%)**.
- **RPO $4.45B** (up from $1.9B a year ago); **net dollar retention 150%** (+1,100 bps QoQ).
- Profitability: **GAAP operating income $754M (46% margin)**; adj. op income $984M (60%); net income $871M (53%).
- **FY26 guide raised to ~$7.7B revenue; US-commercial ~$3.2B (≥120% growth).**
- The thesis read: triple-digit US-commercial growth + 150% NDR = the **data/ontology moat compounding** — exactly the [[Application & software]] selection rule, and structurally insulated from [[Open-weight commoditization (the swing factor)]] (cheaper models = lower input cost for AIP).
- Valuation is the standing bear (richest multiple in software) — a sanity check, not the anchor (valuation never leads the thesis).

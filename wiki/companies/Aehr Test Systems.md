# Aehr Test Systems

**Micro-cap wafer-level burn-in pure-play, pivoting from EV-SiC to AI-processor burn-in.** The speculative end of the [[Semiconductor test (the AI test toll)]] theme.

## Key figures (FY2026, VERIFY)
- Q2 FY26 **bookings $37.2M, book-to-bill >3.5×**; reinstated guidance (**$60-80M H2 FY26 bookings**).
- **Sonoma** production-burn-in win for a **lead hyperscaler's next-gen AI ASIC**; **$14M FOX-XP** order from lead AI-processor customer; follow-on for **AI optical-I/O / interconnect** burn-in.

## The thesis
- **Burn-in is a real, new AI demand vector:** hyperscaler AI ASICs at scale need reliability stress-test (KGD economics — a bad die in a $X00 package/stack is expensive). Aehr's FOX/Sonoma wafer-level systems target exactly this.
- The **EV-SiC → AI pivot is accelerating** (3.5× book-to-bill) — the AI/data-center mix now drives the story.

## Investment read — speculative (high risk/reward)
- **Tiny, lumpy, single-lead-customer concentration**; just whipsawed off EV/SiC dependence. A **venture-grade bet** on the burn-in vector, not a derisked toll like [[Teradyne]]/Advantest.
- Bull: book-to-bill + hyperscaler wins say the pivot is landing. Bear: customer concentration, order lumpiness, micro-cap volatility.

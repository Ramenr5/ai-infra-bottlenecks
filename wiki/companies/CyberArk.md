# CyberArk

**The privileged-access-management (PAM) / machine-identity leader — now part of [[Palo Alto Networks]].** Acquired for **~$25B, closed 2026-02-11** ([[Palo Alto Networks - CyberArk acquisition (2026)]]); its tech is the basis of PANW's **Idira** unified human/machine/agentic identity platform.

## Why it matters
- CyberArk's PAM + **machine-identity** franchise is *exactly* the capability the AI-agent era demands (agents = non-human identities needing privileged-access governance; machine:human → 45-100:1, [[Identity security in the AI era (2026)]]).
- That a $25B acquirer paid up for it is the **clearest single signal that identity is the new perimeter** — the anchor for L5 of the [AI cybersecurity narrative](../../narratives/2026-06-02 AI cybersecurity.md).
- No longer independently investable (absorbed into PANW); exposure is now via [[Palo Alto Networks]].

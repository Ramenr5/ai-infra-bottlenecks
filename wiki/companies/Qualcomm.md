# Qualcomm

**Handset-SoC leader making a speculative datacenter-inference entry.** AI exposure is real at the *edge* but unproven in the datacenter — and it just lost a beachhead to NVIDIA.

## The AI hooks
- **AI200 (2026) / AI250 (2027)** — rack-scale **inference** accelerators on Hexagon NPUs; **768 GB LPDDR per card**, low-TCO LLM/multimodal inference. Ships 2026/27.
- **HUMAIN: 200 MW** deployment in Saudi Arabia starting 2026 (anchor customer).
- The **LPDDR-for-cheap-inference** angle ties to the inference cost-down theme ([[Disaggregated inference (Rubin CPX)]]) — a low-cost inference niche.
- Core: on-device/**edge AI** (NPU in phones/PCs).

## Investment read — low conviction on the AI-infra thesis
- **Late datacenter entrant** vs [[NVIDIA]]/AMD/[[Broadcom]]/hyperscaler-custom; the DC push is small + unproven. Handset-cyclical core dominates.
- **Negative keynote read-through:** [[NVIDIA Computex 2026 Keynote]] showed **N1X + [[MediaTek]] breaking Qualcomm's Windows-on-Arm exclusivity** — a direct hit to its AI-PC beachhead.
- Bull: cheap-inference niche + edge-AI + Saudi anchor. Bear: execution/share risk, WoA loss, handset cyclicality.

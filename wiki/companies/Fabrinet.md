# Fabrinet

**The optical EMS toll — builds the transceivers inside the AI datacenter** (NYSE: FN). The contract-manufacturing layer of [[Networking & interconnect]] optics: the [[Coherent]]/[[Lumentum]]/NVIDIA transceivers are largely *made* by Fabrinet.

## Key figures (2026, VERIFY)
- **Record Q2 2026 revenue ~$1.13B**; **$5.4B revenue by 2028** (target); **lead in the 1.6T transceiver** transition; NVIDIA + Cisco anchor customers.
- **iPronics partnership** → dedicated **silicon-photonics** manufacturing line (operational Q2 2026).
- Decoupling from cyclical telecom → AI-capex primary beneficiary.

## Investment read
- A **structurally advantaged manufacturing toll** — high-precision optical assembly at scale is hard to replicate, and Fabrinet sits across *all* the transceiver brands (architecture-agnostic within optics). Bull: 1.6T → CPO ramp + SiPho line. Bear: EMS margins (assembly economics); customer concentration; optics-architecture exposure (pluggable vs CPO mix).

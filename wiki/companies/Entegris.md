# Entegris

**The recurring-consumables toll** (Nasdaq: ENTG) — specialty materials, microcontamination control (filtration), CMP/planarization, and materials handling. Sells *with every wafer run*, not once — the under-loved layer of [[WFE & process control (the equipment toll)]].

## Key figures (2026, VERIFY)
- ~**$4.6B FY25 revenue**; high-margin specialty chemicals + filtration.
- **AI nodes raise consumable intensity:** HBM + advanced packaging + leading-edge logic = more process steps → more filtration/CMP/materials per wafer.

## Investment read
- **Recurring-revenue toll** (consumables tied to wafer *volume + complexity*, not just equipment cycles) → smoother than WFE tool sales; robust to the silicon war. Bull: rising consumable intensity per AI wafer. Bear: still cyclical to total wafer starts; leverage/integration history.

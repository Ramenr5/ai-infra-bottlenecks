# Soitec

**The substrate chokepoint under silicon photonics** (Euronext Paris: SOI). Its **Photonics-SOI engineered wafers** are what silicon-photonic PICs (transceiver/CPO waveguides, modulators, detectors) are built on — "where silicon photonics begins."

## The thesis
- Every **SiPho transceiver** = a CW laser + a **PIC on a Photonics-SOI wafer**. As AI scale-out goes optical (and toward CPO), the SOI-substrate layer is an indispensable, concentrated link in [[Networking & interconnect]].
- Soitec also has the legacy RF-SOI franchise (handsets) — so AI photonics is a *growth* vector, not the whole company.

## Investment read — Tier 2/3 (architecture-exposed, but broader than one design)
- More **robust than [[Sivers Semiconductors]]**: SOI substrates serve silicon photonics *broadly* (pluggable *and* CPO), not just CPO — so less binary on the CPO-vs-pluggable fork. Still an **optics-architecture-dependent** bet per [[AI value capture across the stack]] (not a Tier-1 silicon-supply-chain toll).
- Caveat: RF-SOI/handset cyclicality dilutes the AI-photonics signal; substrate is a thin slice of the transceiver BOM.

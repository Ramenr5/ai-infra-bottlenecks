# Lasertec

**A near-monopoly *inside* the EUV chain** (TSE: 6920) — EUV photomask inspection. One of the cleanest tolls in the whole supply chain.

## Key figures (2026, VERIFY)
- **~100% share of EUV mask inspection** (no direct competitor); the world's **only commercial actinic inspection** (same 13.5nm wavelength as EUV); **A150 = 100% share at ≤5nm**.
- EUV mask-inspection market **$1.8B (2025) → $4.7B (2034), ~11% CAGR**.

- A **second near-monopoly** sitting *under* [[Lithography monopoly|ASML]]'s EUV monopoly — every EUV mask must be inspected, actinically, and only Lasertec does it. Maximally robust to the silicon war + architecture forks → a Tier-1 [[AI value capture across the stack|cleanest toll]].

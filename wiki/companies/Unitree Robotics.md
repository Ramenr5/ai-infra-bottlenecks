# Unitree Robotics

**China's humanoid volume leader** — the low-cost, ship-now end of the [[Physical AI & robotics]] thesis, and the body behind NVIDIA's Isaac GROOT reference humanoid.

## Key figures (2026, VERIFY — [[Physical AI & humanoid landscape (2026)]])
- Shipped **5,500+ units in 2025**, targeting **10,000–20,000 in 2026** — by units, the clear leader while Western peers are still in pilot.
- Pricing: **G1 from ~$16,000**, full-size **H2 at $29,900** — an order of magnitude cheaper than the capability-first Western approach.
- Body partner for **NVIDIA's Isaac GROOT** reference humanoid ([[NVIDIA Computex 2026 Keynote]]).

## Why it matters
- Embodies the **China-vs-West split**: Unitree/AgiBot scale *units cheaply*; [[Figure AI]]/1X raise on *capability/valuation*. Supply-scale vs IP — a core tension in [[Physical AI & robotics]].
- The **cost-down leader** — sub-$20k humanoids are the Jevons trigger for robot proliferation (cheaper robots → more deployed → more compute demand).
- **Private** (China) — geopolitical/export-control overhang on its access to leading-edge [[NVIDIA]] compute; a reason the China stack may diverge toward domestic silicon.

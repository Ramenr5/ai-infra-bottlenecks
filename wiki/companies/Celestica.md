# Celestica

**North-American EMS/ODM — builds AI servers + hyperscaler networking switches.** The Western analog to the Taiwan ODM cluster ([[Foxconn]], [[Quanta Cloud Technology]]), with a higher-value networking mix.

## Key figures (Q1 2026, VERIFY)
- Revenue **$4.05B (+53% YoY)**; net earnings **$212.3M (+146%)**.
- **HPS (Hardware Platform Solutions) +63% to ~$1.7B** — driven by **hyperscaler networking-switch programs**.
- **Enterprise +101%** — an AI/ML compute program ramp at a hyperscaler.
- **FY26 guide raised to $19B** (from $17B); adj EPS $10.15; FCF $500M.

## The thesis
- Direct **AI-server + scale-out networking** buildout beneficiary; HPS makes it more than a commodity EMS — it builds the **switches** ([[Networking & interconnect]]) and AI-compute boxes.
- Demand is real and accelerating (guide raised, HPS +63%).

## Investment read — cyclical (volume, not rent)
- Per [[AI value capture across the stack]], EMS/ODM is the **thin-margin "volume ≠ rent" tier** (like [[Foxconn]]) — Celestica's HPS mix is higher-value but it's still assembly economics, not a toll.
- **Already re-rated hard** (~33× forward) and **whippy** (shed ~$10B market cap on one print) — the AI re-rate is largely *in*. **Trade the cycle; mind the multiple.**

# Foxconn

Hon Hai Precision (TWSE: 2317) — **the largest manufacturer of NVIDIA AI servers** and the biggest single beneficiary, by revenue, of the physical AI buildout. Assembles GB200/GB300 (and forthcoming [[NVIDIA Computex 2026 Keynote|Vera Rubin]]) racks; named NVIDIA AI-factory partner (via subsidiary [[Ingrasys]]).

## Key facts (verify before acting)

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Q4 2025 revenue | **NT$2.6T (~$83B)**, +22% YoY (Dec quarter; +26% an earlier month) |
| Position | **largest maker of NVIDIA servers**; AI-server revenue the growth driver |
| Datacenter arm | [[Ingrasys]] (NVIDIA AI-factory partner) |
| Customers | NVIDIA + the hyperscalers (server assembly) |

## Why it matters
- **The production layer of the demand thesis:** NVIDIA's $1T Blackwell+Rubin purchase orders ([[NVIDIA Computex 2026 Keynote]]) physically flow through Foxconn's lines. GB200 servers cost ~$2-3M each (~$210B/yr Blackwell-server revenue pool) — Foxconn captures the assembly share.
- A high-revenue, lower-margin read on AI-server *volume* (complement to [[Dell Technologies]] on the OEM side).

## Risks
- Thin EMS margins; revenue ≠ profit leverage
- Customer + NVIDIA concentration; Taiwan/China exposure

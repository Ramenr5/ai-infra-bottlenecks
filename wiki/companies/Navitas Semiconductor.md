# Navitas Semiconductor

**GaN/SiC power-semiconductor pure-play** (Nasdaq: NVTS) — GaNFast (gallium nitride) + GeneSiC. A **semiconductor enabler** of the [[800VDC transition]], now inside NVIDIA's **AI Factory MGX 800VDC ecosystem**.

## The thesis hook — 800V→6V single-stage
- Showcased an **800 V-to-6 V GaNFast Power Delivery Board** at NVIDIA MGX / Computex 2026: **97.5% peak efficiency, 1 MHz, 2100 W/in³**, **16 GaNFast FETs** (650 V/11 mΩ). It **eliminates the 48 V intermediate bus converter** in the compute tray ([[Navitas - 800V-6V PDB at NVIDIA MGX (2026)]]).
- This targets exactly the **on-blade / point-of-load DC-DC socket** that 800VDC-native compute (Phase 2 "Kyber") needs — the density unlock the [[800VDC transition]] tracks.

## Key figures (VERIFY)
- **Q1 FY26 revenue $8.6M** (beat); **EPS −$0.15** (loss-making); raised **$122M** ATM equity for the high-power/AI push.
- Stock re-rated hard (~+24% pre-market) on the NVIDIA association.

## Investment read
- A **high-beta, speculative 800VDC pure-play**: real positioning on a structural theme, but **sub-scale, unprofitable, dilutive** — the opposite end of the risk spectrum from diversified incumbents [[Infineon]] / [[Power Integrations]] (also GaN, also NVIDIA-collaborating) or SiC names [[Wolfspeed]].
- Per [[AI value capture across the stack]] / the 800VDC picks-and-shovels logic: GaN enablers are genuine beneficiaries, but **"MGX showcase ≠ a locked socket at volume"** — design-win conversion + getting to profitability are the gating questions. The "NVIDIA lifts another power-semi stock" dynamic is a sentiment tailwind *and* a froth risk.

## Risks
- Tiny revenue base + losses + ongoing dilution; competes for the same on-blade socket with [[Power Integrations]] (1250V/1700V GaN, NVIDIA-disclosed) and [[Infineon]] (650V GaN + SiC).
- Showcase/ecosystem membership is not a volume contract; NVIDIA-association moves can reverse as fast as they came.

# Advantest

**The purest, biggest AI-test toll** (TSE: 6857; ADR ATEYY) — the dominant supplier of high-end **SoC + memory testers**, and the leader in **HBM** test. The cleaner pure-play than [[Teradyne]] on the [[Semiconductor test (the AI test toll)]] thesis (no robotics/handset dilution).

## Key figures (FY2026 ending Mar 2027, VERIFY)
- **FY26 revenue guide ~$9B (+26% YoY)** from FY25's ~$7.1B.
- **SoC tester market $8.7-9.5B CY26** (from $6.9B CY25, **+32%**) — driven by AI-application volume + complexity.
- **~70% share of AI-chip testing** (reportedly up ~450% in a year — VERIFY; the headline concentration stat).
- Record margins; gaining SoC-tester share; targeting high-end SoC **and** memory dominance.

## The thesis — test intensity is the moat
- **Testing NVIDIA Blackwell-class GPUs + HBM3E/HBM4 requires far longer test times + more sophisticated equipment** — i.e. AI *raises test content per chip*, the core [[Semiconductor test (the AI test toll)]] mechanism. Advantest captures the highest-intensity, highest-value end.
- **HBM test leader** → the most direct [[HBM allocation]] read-through (HBM4 = taller stacks = more KGD/stack test).
- **Robust to the silicon war:** NVIDIA GPU, hyperscaler XPU, AMD — all route through Advantest/Teradyne testers regardless of who wins compute (like [[TSMC]]/memory).

## Investment read
- **Highest-conviction test toll** — concentration (~70% AI test), indispensability (no chip ships untested), and intensity-rising all max. The purer AI-test exposure vs [[Teradyne]].
- **Caveats:** rich valuation after a huge run; **cyclical to chip volume** (a capex pause hits test); guidance prudence has dented shares before. Japan-listed (ADR ATEYY for US access).

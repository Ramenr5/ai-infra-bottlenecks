# LeaderDrive

**China's #1 harmonic-reducer maker and the purest *listed* humanoid-actuator chokepoint** (绿的谐波 / Leader Harmonious Drive; SSE STAR: **688017**). The standout of the [[Goldman - Humanoid robot ecosystem map (2026)]] — it spans **all three actuator categories** (rotary reducers + linear/roller screws + dexterous-hand actuators).

## Key figures (VERIFY)
- 2025: revenue **570.7M RMB (+47%)**, net profit **124.4M (>2×)**; market cap **~57.7B RMB** → **~100× sales** (priced for the humanoid future, not current fundamentals).
- **China harmonic-reducer share ~30-40%** (J.P. Morgan) — the **#1** maker. *(The often-quoted ">60%" is a 2020 figure / robot-specific; share has drifted down under domestic competition.)*
- Products: harmonic (strain-wave) reducers, rotary-joint reducers, frameless torque motors, **entering planetary roller screws** (hip/knee high-torque joints).
- Customers: **AGIBOT, UBTech** (confirmed), Universal Robots (likely); **Tesla, Figure = *potential*, not confirmed**.

## Goldman exposure ranking (Exhibit 5)
LeaderDrive sits in Goldman's **#1 component category — harmonic reduction gear (exposure score 16)** — *and* planetary roller screw (14). Harmonic ranks top for high content-value + high tech/mfg barrier + high humanoid-incremental opportunity (the value-dense chokepoint).

## The thesis — strong theme, high-risk vehicle
- **Bull:** content-per-robot, **agnostic to which humanoid maker wins** ("a substantial share in every produced robot"); China's scalable-mass-production cost edge that Western incumbents (Japan's [[Harmonic Drive Systems]]) can't match; multi-actuator breadth + roller-screw expansion grows BOM share. A **directional long on humanoid units shipped** (un-modelable; option-like).
- **Bear:** **~100× sales** = extreme multiple risk if mass production slips ([[Physical AI & robotics]]: *option value, not order book*); **share eroding** (60%→30-40%) under domestic competition (Shuanghuan, Reach Machinery, Zhongda Leader); **China A-share** (foreign-access/liquidity/geopolitical); roller-screw incumbents (Wuzhou New Spring, [[Schaeffler]]).
- **Net:** highest-conviction humanoid-actuator *theme*; size as the **high-beta call option** it is.
